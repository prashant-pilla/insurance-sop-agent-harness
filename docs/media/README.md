# Media

- `demo.gif`: the policyholder "Demo caller" conversation end to end, embedded at the top of the top-level README. Margaret Chen's opening message verifies identity and resolves the claim in one turn, the agent explains the denial of CL-2048, answers "What documents do I need to send and by when?", wraps up, and sends the summary email after consent; the SOP State panel steps from `VERIFY_ID` to `CLOSED` alongside. The 40 s recording is played at 2.2x (about 18 s), 1200 px wide, 10 fps, no audio. Made with ffmpeg: `setpts=PTS/2.2,fps=10,scale=1200:-1:flags=lanczos` into a two-pass `palettegen=stats_mode=diff` / `paletteuse=dither=bayer:bayer_scale=5:diff_mode=rectangle` GIF.
- `demo.mp4`: the same sped-up recording as h264 (crf 28, faststart), a smaller and sharper fallback for places that accept video rather than GIF. Not referenced from the README.
- `demo.png`: still from the same flow, used for the repository social preview. Identity verified on one turn (name, DOB, ID last 4 checked), phase `PROCESS_CASE`, agent explains the denial of claim CL-2048 and answers "What documents do I need to send and by when?".
- `representative.png`: "Representative caller" flow. David Chen calls for his mother; the controller finds the authorization, requests consent, and only discusses the claim after consent is approved (state panel shows the representative line and consent status).
